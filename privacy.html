Salt Heart — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Developer: Make The Best Games · Contact: thinguyendinh83@gmail.com

Salt Heart ("the game") is a free single-player pirate tap game for Android, published on Google Play, and for iPhone and iPad, published on the App Store, by Make The Best Games ("we", "us"). It has optional in-app purchases and optional rewarded video ads. This policy explains what data the game and the services built into it handle, why, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the game app com.makethebestgames.saltheart on Android, iPhone and iPad; where the two differ, this page says so.

1. No account, no personal profile kept by us

  • The game has no sign-in and no account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device: the save file, the order IDs of purchases already granted, your answer to the ad privacy question, and your settings (sound, language, graphics quality, effects). If you use the save code in Settings, the code stays wherever you paste it; we never receive it.
  • We run no server for the game and receive no data from it directly. Usage statistics go to Google Analytics for Firebase (section 2); we only see aggregated reports in the Firebase console. Leaderboards and tournaments in the game are simulated on your device; no data about you is sent to other players.

3. Your choices

  • Personalised ads: before the first video the game asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your answer any time in Settings > Personalized ads. "Non-personalised ads only" means the ad partner shows ads without building an advertising profile and does not sell or share your data for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • Apple's tracking question (iPhone and iPad): only if you choose "Allow personalised ads" in the game's question, iOS then shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency question, which asks whether Salt Heart may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. The ad partner reads the IDFA only if you tap Allow. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, or choose "Non-personalised ads only" (then the question does not appear), the IDFA is not shared, and the videos still work. iOS shows the question only once; if you first choose non-personalised ads and later allow personalised ads in Settings > Personalized ads, it appears then.
  • No videos at all: switch off Settings > Parrot videos. Videos are always optional; the game can be played fully without watching any.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, you can reset or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (wording depends on the device). On iPhone and iPad, open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and switch off Salt Heart, or turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track for every app; the game then gets no IDFA.
  • Usage statistics: advertising uses of the statistics (ad storage, ad user data, ad personalisation) follow your answer in Settings > Personalized ads. To start over with a new statistics ID, clear the game's data in Android Settings > Apps > Salt Heart > Storage, or on iPhone and iPad delete the app and install it again (either way this also deletes your save on this device).
  • Purchases and refunds: Settings > Purchases lists your latest purchases with their order IDs and can restore a purchase that did not arrive. Refunds are handled by Google Play on Android and by Apple on iPhone and iPad; see our Refund Policy.

4. Legal bases (EU, UK and similar laws)

  • Your consent — personalised ads and, on iPhone and iPad, tracking through the IDFA. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings > Personalized ads, and on iPhone and iPad also in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Performing our contract with you — granting the items you buy.
  • Legitimate interests — non-personalised ads that keep the game free, fraud prevention, crash diagnostics to keep the game working, and usage statistics to improve the game.

Our partners may process data on servers outside your country (for example in the United States). They use the safeguards required by law, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Your rights and how to delete your data

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK GDPR, Vietnam's Decree 13/2023/ND-CP on personal data protection, or US state privacy laws), you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict its use, withdraw consent, and complain to your data protection authority.

  • Data on your device (save, purchase records, ad answer, settings): uninstall the game or clear its data in Android Settings > Apps > Salt Heart > Storage; on iPhone and iPad, delete the app (touch and hold its icon, then Remove App > Delete App). This deletes it at once.
  • Data held by our partners (Google, Unity): email us at thinguyendinh83@gmail.com with the subject "Salt Heart data request". Because we hold no account for you, please include your Advertising ID (Android Settings > Privacy > Ads) so the partners can find the data linked to it; iPhone and iPad do not show the IDFA, so there tell us your device model instead. For usage statistics, also tell us roughly when you played, and we will ask Google to delete the data of the matching app instance. We reply within 3 working days and forward the request to the partners, who complete it under their own policies (usually within 30 days). You can also contact Google and Unity directly through the links in section 2.
  • App Store purchase data is held by Apple; you can ask Apple for it or for its deletion at https://privacy.apple.com.

6. Children

Salt Heart is meant for players 13 and older and is not directed at children. The ad partner is told the app is not child-directed. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will help remove any data we can reach through our partners.

7. Data retention

  • Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game (on iPhone and iPad: delete the app) or clear its data.
  • Data held by Google, Apple and Unity is kept for the periods set in their policies. Usage statistics in Google Analytics for Firebase are kept for the retention period set in our Analytics account (at most 14 months).
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer them, then deleted.

8. Security

All communication of the services above uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The game does not request permissions for your contacts, camera, microphone, files or precise location. On iPhone and iPad, the only system question it may show is Apple's tracking question (section 3).

9. Changes

We will update this page when the game adds or removes a service or changes how data is used. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will also be noted in the game's update notes on Google Play and the App Store.
