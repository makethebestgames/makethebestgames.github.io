Salt Heart — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026 · Developer: Make The Best Games · Contact: thinguyendinh83@gmail.com

Salt Heart ("the game") is a free single-player game for Android with optional in-app purchases and optional rewarded video ads. This policy explains what data the game and the services built into it handle, and the choices you have.

1. No account, no personal profile kept by us

  • The game has no sign-in and no account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device. If you use "Copy save code" in Settings, the code stays wherever you paste it; we never receive it.
  • We (the developer) run no server for the game and receive no data from it directly.

2. Data handled by services inside the game

The game includes these third-party services. They act under their own privacy policies.

Service Why Data it may collect
Google Play Billing (through Unity In-App Purchasing) Selling pearl chests Purchase details (product, time, order id) are processed by Google. The game keeps the order ids of purchases it has granted on your device so a purchase is never granted twice. Payment details are handled only by Google; we never see them.
Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation) with Unity Ads Optional rewarded videos for the big parrot gifts Advertising ID (or App Set ID), device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), approximate location derived from the IP address, ad interactions (views, clicks, rewards), diagnostics (crash and performance data of the ad SDK). Used to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalise ads.

Links: Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy · Unity Privacy Policy — https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy · Unity LevelPlay / ironSource privacy — https://www.is.com/privacy-policy/ (check the current links before publishing).

3. Your choices

  • Personalised ads: before the first video the game asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your answer any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. "Non-personalised ads only" means the ad partner shows ads without building an advertising profile and does not sell or share your data for cross-context advertising (CCPA / US state laws).
  • No videos at all: switch off Settings > Parrot videos. Buying any pearl chest also turns the videos off.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Videos are always optional; the game can be played fully without watching any.
  • Purchases and refunds: Settings > Purchase support lists your latest purchases with their order IDs and can restore a purchase that did not arrive. Refunds are handled by Google Play; see our Refund Policy at https://makethebestgames.github.io/refund.html.

4. Children

Salt Heart is meant for players 13 and older and is not directed at children. The ad partner is told the app is not child-directed. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will help remove any data we can reach through our partners.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device (save, purchase records, ad answer) is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Data held by Google, Unity or LevelPlay follows their policies; you can contact them, or us at thinguyendinh83@gmail.com, and we will forward deletion requests for the identifiers our partners hold.

6. Security

All communication of the services above uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

7. Changes

We will update this page when the game adds or removes a service. The date at the top shows the latest version.
