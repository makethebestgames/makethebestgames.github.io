MakeTheBestGames Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026.

1. Who we are

MakeTheBestGames ("we", "us") makes mobile games. This policy covers every game we publish on Google Play and the App Store (the "Services"). We decide how your data is used in our games, so we are the data controller. We do not sell your personal information.

Website: https://makethebestgames.github.io

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • The player name you type for the rankings. Other players can see it.
  • What you write in an email to us, if you contact us.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your real name, address or phone number.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, screen and performance data.
  • Identifiers: a random player ID, an app instance ID, and your advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iPhone when you allow tracking).
  • Usage information: game events (for example a stage reached or a screen opened), session length, ad views and taps, app version.
  • Approximate location: worked out from your IP address. We do not read your GPS location.
  • Purchase information: the item you bought and an order ID. We never see your card details.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: crash reports and performance data.

Your game progress, settings and unlocked purchases are also kept in a save file on your phone. Deleting the app deletes this file.

2.3 Information from third parties

If you use cloud save, your Google Play Games account (Android) or Game Center account (iPhone) stores your progress. We receive the saved progress, not your account password. Our ad and analytics partners (section 6) also send us reports about ads and how the games are used.

4. Storage and retention

We keep data only as long as we need it for the purposes above. Analytics data is kept for at most 14 months. Ranking data stays until you ask us to delete it or the board is reset. Our partners keep data under their own policies (section 6). Data may be processed in countries other than yours, such as the United States, where our partners operate.

5. Sharing information

We share data only in these cases:

  • With the service providers listed in section 6, so they can do their work for us.
  • With advertising partners, for ads, if you allow it where the law asks for your choice.
  • With other players: only your player name and score on the rankings.
  • With authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and users.
  • If our business is sold or merged, the data may move to the new owner under this policy.

Advertising IDs and your ad choices

Our games show ads. Ad partners use an advertising ID to show and measure ads. When a game first asks, you can choose personalised or non-personalised ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland a consent form is shown. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads). On iPhone, the game asks for tracking permission (ATT) and uses the IDFA only if you allow it. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

7. Social features

The games have rankings. Your player name and score are visible to other players. There is no chat. Use a name that is not your real name if you do not want to be identified. The games do not post to social networks for you.

8. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your data, and our partners use encrypted connections. No method of storing or sending data is fully secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

10. Push notifications

Our games may remind you of things in the game, such as a finished task. These reminders are local to your phone and are not sent from our servers. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

11. Payments

Purchases are made through Google Play or the App Store. They process your payment, and we never see or store your card details. Virtual items and currencies are not refundable, except where the store's rules or the law say otherwise. For a refund, contact Google Play or Apple.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland, Brazil or California), you may have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is wrong.
  • Delete your data.
  • Restrict or object to some processing, and withdraw consent at any time.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To use a right, email us (section 15) from the device owner's address and tell us your player name. We may ask for proof that the data is yours. For ranking data we will ask Unity to delete it. We will not treat you worse for using your rights.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we change the effective date at the top. For big changes we will also tell you in the game or on our website.

14. Addendum for California residents

This section applies under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

  • What we collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (advertising ID, player ID), internet and app activity, approximate location, and purchase history, as described in section 2.
  • Why and with whom: for the purposes in section 3, shared with the providers in section 6.
  • No sale for money. Sharing an advertising ID with ad partners for ads can count as "sharing" for cross-context advertising in California. To opt out, choose non-personalised ads when the game asks, or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Your rights: to know, delete, correct and opt out, as in section 12. We do not discriminate against you for using them.
  • Sensitive information: we do not collect it.
